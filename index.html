Casey Blacker



I am an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Augusta University. My interests include moment maps, Lie groupoids, bundle gerbes, and quantization. More recently, I have become interested in higher geometry, including (higher) stacks and gerbes.

I also serve on the Augusta University Faculty Senate, representing the Department of Mathematics.
